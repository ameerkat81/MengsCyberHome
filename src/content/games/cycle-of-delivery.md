  <div class="subsection__aside">
    <h3 class="subsection__title">Why this story?</h3>
    <div class="subsection__text">
      <p>I used to work as an iOS developer at a large tech company.</p>
      <p>There, I experienced what it's like to be "just another cog in the machine", such as spending a whole day having meetings in a small meeting room or developing features that were predefined.</p>
      <p>As I felt increasingly detached from my own work and life in Beijing, I also became more aware of my role as a worker within this system, which led me to notice the labor of others that often goes unseen, like delivery riders and couriers.</p>
      <p>Through my games, I want to tell their stories — and mine.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="story-duo">
  <div class="story-duo__column story-duo__column--theme">
    <h3 class="story-duo__title">Theme</h3>
    <div class="story-duo__text">
      <p>Behind the platform's efficiency, people are shaped into replaceable units by algorithms.</p>
      <p>Follow Amanda, an algorithm designer, and Lin, a delivery rider, as they struggle with rules, expectations, and personal desires within the same system.</p>
      <p>It's not about defeating the system, but staying aware within it — recognizing one's own desire before the system rewrites it.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="story-duo__column story-duo__column--overview">
    <h3 class="story-duo__title">Story Overview</h3>
    <div class="story-duo__text">
      <p>Amanda is tasked with optimizing dispatch efficiency, while Lin strives to earn high rankings and income through long hours and hard work.</p>
      <p>As algorithms evolve, Amanda's decisions tighten control over the system, while Lin pushes himself to keep up, risking his health.</p>
      <p>They never met before the final act, but their actions affect each other indirectly through the shared delivery system.</p>
      <p>Rising tensions lead to riders resisting and protesting against the system's pressures.</p>
      <p>In the final act, Amanda uncovers evidence of exploitation and shares it with Lin.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="subsection subsection--half">
  <div class="subsection__aside">
    <h3 class="subsection__title">Five-Act Narrative Structure</h3>
    <div class="subsection__text">
      <p>The game unfolds over five acts, with each choice affecting an "Inner Conflict" value.</p>
      <p>In Act 4, the awakening option appears only if the "Inner Conflict" value reaches a threshold.</p>
      <p>Only choosing the awake option breaks the loop—otherwise, the story resets to Act 1.</p>
    </div>
  </div>

<div class="subsection subsection--half">
  <div class="subsection__aside">
    <h3 class="subsection__title">Why this Structure?</h3>
    <div class="subsection__text">
      <p>I designed the loop because, to me, being trapped in a system feels like living inside an endless cycle — days repeat, rules reinforce themselves, and little truly changes.</p>
      <p>This structure reflects one thing I've learnt: only by being aware of the system, questioning it, and choosing to step outside, does the loop end.</p>
    </div>

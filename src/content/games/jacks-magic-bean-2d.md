**Time:** 2018.1.5 – 2018.6.10  
**Role:** Programmer, Co-Designer  
**Platform:** iOS (SpriteKit, Objective-C)

Developed as an undergraduate team project. I contributed to programming and co-design, building the action and narrative flow on iOS with SpriteKit.
